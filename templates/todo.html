🌍 Smart Travel Planner

One Platform for Complete Travel Planning

📌 Project Overview

Smart Travel Planner is an AI-powered travel booking platform where users can plan an entire trip by simply entering their destination, travel dates, and budget. The application automatically calculates travel costs, suggests the best itinerary, books hotels, transportation, restaurants, and local activities.
